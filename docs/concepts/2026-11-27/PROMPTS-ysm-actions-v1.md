## Sheet 1

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a multi-angle action concept sheet for later editing our existing Yes Steve Model (YSM) avatar.
Reference 1 is the authoritative web-model-derived turnaround: preserve its geometry language, hair silhouette, slim long-legged proportions and clothing. Reference 2 defines the approved quiet/intense personality and full-body voxel style. Reference 3 defines the small red spiral pixel eyes and restrained mouth. DO NOT copy the knife from reference 2; these are unarmed YSM locomotion poses.
Lock the SAME Satou avatar across all panels: simple pink twin buns and ahoge, broad angular bangs, flat tapering layered pink side/back hair ending near the waist; no animal ears, tail or floating hair curl. Navy school blazer with narrow pale/gold trim and striped cuffs, dusty brown-rose buttoned vest, white collar, prominent red chest bow, green pleated skirt, dark violet knee socks, brown block shoes. Small square burgundy/red spiral eyes when visible, tiny neutral mouth and composed stare. Rectangular block hands without individual fingers. Crisp square pixel texture patches on broad low-poly/cuboid surfaces, not 2D anime, smooth high-poly anatomy, or hundreds of tiny stacked cubes.
Layout: high-resolution LANDSCAPE 3 columns x 2 rows, exactly SIX complete full-body renders. Top row = one action frozen at the same phase, viewed from FRONT 3/4 (40 degrees), SIDE (90 degrees), BACK 3/4 (140 degrees). Bottom row = a second action, frozen at the same phase, from the same three camera angles. Columns must show genuine orientation changes with the camera at normal eye level. Preserve the same limb configuration between the three views of each row, as if rotating one posed 3D mesh. Show complete buns, hands and shoes, generous spacing, consistent body scale and readable silhouettes. Back views show the actual back hair, not a face painted on the back of the head. Keep the skirt normally draped and the framing neutral.
Use the actual YSM action descriptions below as the pose guide, not generic fashion/photo poses. The intent is to examine clothing, hair, elbows, knees and silhouette from several angles before modifying the model. No weapons, added accessories, other characters, UI, watermark, speed lines or extra props. Muted cool mauve-lavender studio backdrop, soft grounding shadows and a restrained burgundy edge tone; preserve readable original outfit colors. Tiny captions under each panel, no large blocks of technical text.

Title "SATOU — YSM IDLE / WALK".
TOP ROW: actual YSM clip "idle", sampled near 1.0 s. Neutral relaxed standing, arms down at sides with slight outward shoulder rest, elbows almost straight, both legs nearly straight and only subtly separated, torso upright, quiet forward gaze. Not hand-on-hip, waving or a model pose.
BOTTOM ROW: actual YSM clip "walk", sampled near 0.125 s of its 1 s cycle. Natural moderate stepping phase, one thigh about 23 degrees forward and opposite thigh about 19 degrees back, knees softly bent about 23 degrees, arms counter-swing modestly with elbows around 20–25 degrees, torso mostly upright. One foot advances while the other pushes off. NOT running, jumping or an exaggerated runway stride.
Captions in reading order: "IDLE — FRONT 3/4", "IDLE — SIDE", "IDLE — BACK 3/4", "WALK — FRONT 3/4", "WALK — SIDE", "WALK — BACK 3/4".

Interpret the intended grounded folded-leg/kneeling sit: lower legs tucked under/behind the body, torso upright with a modest lean, block hands resting together in front. This is NOT sitting on an invented chair and NOT legs stretched straight forward. The skirt stays modestly draped over the folded upper legs, no underside view. The back and side must show the tucked-leg silhouette and back hair naturally settling.
Captions in reading order: "CROUCH — FRONT 3/4", "CROUCH — SIDE", "CROUCH — BACK 3/4", "SIT — FRONT 3/4", "SIT — SIDE", "SIT — BACK 3/4".
